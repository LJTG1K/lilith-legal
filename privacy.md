# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

LILITH is a private internal tool operated by Taylorcom ("we", "us"). It is used only by our own team to manage our own advertising accounts and business data. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up.

## What we collect and use

- **Advertising data from our own Meta ad accounts**, such as campaigns, ad sets, ads, spend, impressions, clicks and purchase figures. We read this through the Meta Marketing API to report on and plan our own advertising.
- **Ad content we create**, such as ad names, text, links and images, which we send to our own ad accounts through the Meta Marketing API.
- **Business data from our own online stores**, such as orders and stock levels, used for internal reporting.
- **Team sign-in details**: the email addresses of the few people allowed to use the tool, and a log of their sign-ins and actions.

We do not collect personal data from the public, we do not run tracking on visitors through this tool, and we do not receive or store the personal data of Meta users who see or interact with our ads beyond the aggregated figures Meta reports to us.

## How we use it

Only to run and measure our own advertising and business. We do not sell data, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers (a hosted database service) under access controls that limit it to approved team members. Access tokens for Meta and other services are kept in private configuration and are never published.

## Retention and deletion

We keep advertising and reporting data for as long as it is useful to the business. You can ask us to delete any personal data we hold about you by emailing lachlan.j.ta@gmail.com. We will act on a deletion request within 30 days, and our data deletion instructions are the same: email us at that address with the subject "Data deletion".

## Meta platform data

Our use of data received from Meta follows Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies. If you remove our app's access to your Meta account or assets, we stop using that data.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
